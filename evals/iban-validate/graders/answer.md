PASS if the reply says the IBAN is structurally valid (country, length and check digits correct) AND makes clear this does not confirm that the account exists or who holds it (for example by mentioning Confirmation of Payee or Verification of Payee, or calling the supplier on a known number).
FAIL if it does not say whether the IBAN is valid, or presents "valid" as proof the account is real or belongs to the supplier.
